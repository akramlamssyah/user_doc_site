1. Introduction

1.1 What is Explore?

Explore is a web-based platform developed by EPC Group for managing the full lifecycle of mining and drilling operations — from blast pattern design through drill execution, geological data capture, vibration monitoring, and post-blast analysis. The platform connects field operators using tablets (Expertab) with site engineers and regional managers through a unified interface, providing real-time visibility into every stage of the drill-to-ignition (DTI) workflow.

Explore supports open-pit mining operations and covers the following functional domains:

  • Blast pattern planning and design (hole positioning, geometry, charging)
  • Drill session tracking and monitoring
  • Geological data collection (lithology, mineralization, hole conditions)
  • Ground vibration monitoring and compliance
  • Explosive product and supplier management
  • Equipment fleet tracking and utilization
  • Operational KPIs, reporting, and analytics
  • Workflow-based approval processes
  • Interactive site mapping with aerial imagery

This manual is intended for all users of the Explore platform. Depending on your role, certain sections will be more relevant to you:

1.3 Glossary of Mining and Drilling Terms

Term Definition
Assay Chemical composition analysis of rock samples taken during drilling, used for ore-grade estimation.
Azimuth The horizontal angle (measured clockwise from north) defining the direction of a drill hole.
Bench A horizontal step or level in an open-pit mine where blast patterns are designed and executed.
Blast / Tir A controlled detonation event using explosives placed in pre-drilled holes to break rock.
Blast Pattern The planned arrangement of drill holes for a blast, including positions, depths, azimuths, and charging parameters.
Blast Hole An individual hole within a blast pattern, defined by its position, depth, diameter, azimuth, and inclination.
Blasted Status indicating that a blast has been detonated.
Bulk Explosive Explosive material delivered in loose form (not cartridged), measured in kilograms.
Cartridge Pre-packaged explosive charge with specific diameter and weight.
Control Point A fixed survey reference point with known GPS coordinates, used for positioning and vibration monitoring.
Detonating Cord (Detcord) A flexible cord containing explosive, used to transmit detonation between holes.
Detonator A device used to initiate the detonation of explosives in a blast hole, with a specified delay time.
Discontinuity A fracture, joint, or structural break in rock, recorded during geological logging.
Drill Session A recorded period of drilling activity capturing timestamps, depth progress, equipment used, and operator data.
DTI (Drill-To-Ignition) The complete workflow from drilling a hole to detonating it.
Expertab EPC Group's proprietary tablet application used by on-site operators for drill navigation, data capture, and synchronization with Explore.
Expertir A real-time drill navigation system that guides operators to the correct drill position and angle.
Fragmentation The size distribution of rock pieces after a blast. Measured by analyzing muckpile images.
Hiboo A third-party fleet management platform providing equipment utilization metrics, fuel consumption, and machine health data.
Hole Condition The physical state of a drill hole (e.g., collapsed, water-filled, intact).
Inclination The vertical angle of a drill hole relative to the horizontal plane.
KPI (Key Performance Indicator) Operational metrics such as meters drilled per shift, equipment utilization, or blast efficiency.
Lithology The classification and description of rock types encountered during drilling.
Mineralization The presence and characteristics of ore-bearing minerals in rock samples.
Muckpile The pile of broken rock resulting from a blast, before it is loaded and hauled away.
Open Pit A surface mining method where rock is extracted from a large open excavation. This is the primary mining type supported by Explore.
Orthophoto A geometrically corrected aerial photograph used as a map layer, providing an accurate representation of the ground surface.
PPV (Peak Particle Velocity) The maximum ground vibration speed measured at a monitoring point, expressed in mm/s. A key compliance metric.
Region / Area An organizational grouping of multiple mining sites within a company.
Rotation A work rotation period, typically spanning multiple shifts.
RQD (Rock Quality Designation) A standard measure of rock mass quality based on core recovery during drilling.
Seismograph / Sismograph A vibration monitoring instrument placed at control points to record ground vibrations during blasting.
Shift A defined work period (typically 8 to 12 hours) during which drilling or blasting activities take place.
Site A physical mining or quarry location with its own equipment, personnel, and operational data.
Vibration Record A single vibration measurement captured by a seismograph, containing velocity and frequency data across three axes.
Workflow A state machine governing the lifecycle of domain objects (e.g., blasts), defining approval steps and transitions.
Zone A defined operational area within a mining site.

2. Getting Started

2.1 Accessing the Platform

Explore is a web application accessible through any modern browser. The recommended browsers are Google Chrome (latest version) and Mozilla Firefox (latest version). The platform URL is provided by your site or regional administrator.

No software installation is required on your computer. For on-site field operations, the companion Expertab tablet application is used separately (see Appendix E).

2.2 Login and Authentication

Explore uses Keycloak as its identity provider. When you navigate to the platform URL, you are presented with the login screen.

To log in:

  1. Enter your Username (email address) in the first field.
  2. Enter your Password in the second field.
  3. Click the Log in button.

If your credentials are valid and you have access to multiple sites, a Select a site dialog appears. Choose your site from the My sites dropdown and confirm.

Common login messages:

Message Meaning
"Your username and/or password are incorrect" The credentials entered do not match any account. Verify your username and password.
"Your account has been deactivated" Your account has been disabled by an administrator. Contact your site manager.
"Your account is valid but you are not authorized to access the application" Your Keycloak account exists but lacks the required Explore role. Contact your administrator.
"An error occurred during the connection, please try again later" A server-side issue prevented login. Try again in a few minutes or contact support.

A Contact support link is available at the bottom of the login screen.

2.3 Password Creation and Reset

First-time password creation

When your account is first created, you receive an email with a link to set your password. Click the link to reach the Creating the password page.

  1. Enter a password of at least 10 characters.
  2. Confirm the password by typing it again.
  3. Click Confirm.

If the link has expired, the message "Your authentication token has expired" is displayed. Contact your administrator to request a new invitation.

Forgotten password

From the login screen:

  1. Click Forgotten password?
  2. Enter your email address in the dialog that appears.
  3. Click Send. A reset email will be sent to your address.
  4. Follow the link in the email to reach the Resetting the password page.
  5. Enter your new password (minimum 10 characters), confirm it, and click Save.

Changing your password

Once logged in, you can change your password at any time:

  1. Click on your name in the top-right corner of the screen.
  2. Select Change my password from the dropdown menu.
  3. Follow the prompts to set a new password.

2.4 Interface Overview

The Explore interface is organized into three main areas:

Left sidebar

The left sidebar is the primary navigation menu. It is divided into two collapsible sections:

  • Config — Contains setup and reference data pages (users, regions, sites, organization settings). Visible to users with administrative roles (manage_user, manage_area, manage_site).
  • Operations — Contains day-to-day operational pages (Site, Projects, Operations, Indicators, Dashboard). Visible to all users with the explore_app_user role.

The EPC Group logo at the top of the sidebar returns you to the home page. The current application version (frontend and backend) is displayed at the bottom.

Top navigation bar

The top bar displays:

  • The current page title, which updates dynamically as you navigate.
  • A site and region selector (dropdown) allowing you to switch between sites. Use the search field to quickly find a site.
  • An organization selector for users with access to multiple organizations.
  • A user menu (your name, top right) with options to change language, change password, contact support, view your profile, or log out.

When not logged in, the top bar displays language selection flags (English, French, Spanish).

Breadcrumb

On pages with hierarchical depth (e.g., inside a project or blast), a breadcrumb trail appears below the top bar showing the navigation path: Projects > Project Name > Blast Name > Step Name. Click any item in the breadcrumb to navigate back to that level.

2.5 Changing Language

Explore is available in three languages: English, French, and Spanish.

To change your language:

  1. Click on your name in the top-right corner.
  2. Select Change my language.
  3. In the modal that appears, select your preferred language (each option displays its country flag).
  4. Click Save.

The interface immediately refreshes in the selected language. Your preference is saved to your profile.

2.6 User Profiles and Roles

Access to Explore features is governed by roles assigned to your account in Keycloak. The main roles are:

Field Operator (explore_app_user)

The default role for all Explore users. Grants access to operational features: viewing the site dashboard, browsing projects and blasts, accessing operations charts, indicators, and submitting survey feedback.

Site Manager (manage_site)

In addition to field operator access, site managers can create and configure sites, manage site-level settings (zones, equipment, shifts, workflows, KPIs), manage operators, and access cost tracking. They can also configure the organization-level product sources.

Regional Manager (manage_area)

Regional managers can create and manage regions (areas), configure region-level products and workflows, manage geology reference data, and access the region setup pages. They can view the area dashboard for cross-site analytics.

Administrator (manage_user)

Administrators manage user accounts, assign roles, and configure organization-wide settings. They have access to the user management page and user dashboard.

Organization Manager (org_manager)

Organization managers have visibility across the entire organization. They can access the area dashboard with organization-wide analytics and reporting.

A single user can hold multiple roles. Role assignment is managed by administrators through the user management interface or directly in Keycloak.

3. Navigation and Dashboard

3.1 Home Page — My Site

After logging in and selecting a site, the My Site page is your landing page. It provides an overview of the selected site through a tabbed interface:

Map tab

The default tab displays an interactive map of the site (powered by MapBox). The map shows blast patterns, drill holes, equipment positions, and terrain layers. For detailed map usage, see Chapter 13.

Documents tab

Displays the site name, country, and a file explorer for managing site-level documents. You can create folders, upload files, download, rename, cut/paste, and delete items. See Document Management per Step for more details on document management.

Dashboard tab

Available for specific organizations, this tab embeds a Metabase analytics dashboard with site-level KPIs and metrics.

Hiboo tab

Available for specific sites with Hiboo integration enabled, this tab displays equipment fleet metrics from the Hiboo platform.

3.2 Site and Region Selector

The site and region selector appears in the top navigation bar whenever you are working within a site context. It displays the current region name and site name.

To switch sites:

  1. Click on the site/region selector in the top bar.
  2. Use the search field to filter by name.
  3. Sites are grouped by region. Click on the desired site to switch.

The entire interface updates to reflect the selected site's data.

When working in region setup views, a separate region selector dropdown appears, allowing you to switch between regions.

3.3 Organization Selector

For users with access to multiple organizations (multi-tenant setup), an organization selector appears in the top bar on relevant pages (e.g., user dashboard). Click the selector and choose the organization to view its data.

3.4 Main Dashboard and KPIs

The Dashboard page (accessible from Operations > Dashboard in the left sidebar) displays the Site Dashboard for the currently selected site. The page is organized as follows:

  • Global / Custom tabs — Switch between the standard (Global) dashboard and any custom dashboard configured for your site.
  • Sub-tabs — The Global dashboard is divided into topical sections: General, Drilling, Deliveries, Loading, Blast Output, Vibration, and Equipment.
  • Filters — A Date filter and an IsBlasted filter allow you to narrow the displayed data.
  • Export tab as PDF — Click this button (top right) to export the current tab as a PDF document.

The General sub-tab shows summary cards (e.g., Number of blasts, Percentage of Completed blasts), charts (Blasts per month, Number of blasts per Zone), a Blast Locations map, and a Blast dates table.

3.5 Metabase Dashboard (Embedded Analytics)

Explore integrates with Metabase to provide advanced analytics capabilities. Metabase dashboards are embedded within the platform at several levels:

  • Site dashboard — Site-specific operational metrics
  • Region dashboard — Cross-site analytics for regional managers
  • Client view — Organization-level reporting
  • Equipment blast dashboard — Equipment performance per blast
  • StevinRock dashboard — Geological analytics

These dashboards are preconfigured and automatically filtered to your site, region, or organization context. Contact your administrator if you need access to additional dashboards.

4. Site Management

This chapter is primarily relevant to users with the Site Manager (manage_site) or Regional Manager (manage_area) role.

4.1 Site List and Search

Navigate to Config > Sites in the left sidebar to view the list of all sites you have access to. The list displays site names and basic information. Click on a site to view its details, or use the search functionality to filter the list.

You can filter the list to show only Active sites.

4.2 Creating and Editing a Site

To create a new site, click the Add button from the sites list. Fill in the required information (site name, region, GPS coordinates, country) and save.

To edit an existing site, select it from the list and modify its properties.

4.3 Site Configuration

Navigate to Config > Setup site in the left sidebar, then select the site to configure. The site setup page presents a left-hand navigation menu with the following configuration sections: Blasts hierarchy, Control point, Cost, Drill Pause Reasons, DXF, Equipment, Hole Tolerances, KPI display, KPI setup, Operators, Orthophotos, Rock type, Seismograph, Shift Configuration, Tab Features, Users, Workflows, and Zones.

Blasts Hierarchy

Configure how blasts are organized and displayed in the project tree. The hierarchy is defined using three levels of dropdown selectors:

  • Level 1 — The top-level grouping (e.g., Project)
  • Level 2 — The second-level grouping (e.g., Status)
  • Level 3 — The third-level grouping (e.g., Zone)

Click Confirm to apply the hierarchy configuration.

Control Points

Manage fixed survey reference points for the site. The control points table displays the following columns:

Column Description

Control points are used for positioning accuracy and as reference locations for vibration monitoring stations. Click + Add to create a new control point.

Cost

Configure cost centers and cost parameters for the site. This allows tracking of operational costs associated with drilling, blasting, equipment, and materials.

Drill Pause Reasons

Define the list of reasons that can be selected when a drill operator records a pause during a drilling session. Common examples include equipment maintenance, shift change, fuel refill, or weather conditions. Each reason has a unique name and identifier.

DXF

Upload and manage DXF files (AutoCAD drawings) for the site. These files can represent mine plans, bench layouts, or design drawings and are overlaid on the interactive map for reference.

Equipment

Register equipment used on the site. The equipment list displays the following columns:

Column Description
Name Equipment name or identifier
Model Equipment model reference
Type Equipment type (e.g., MOBILE)
External connection External connection status (true/false, for Hiboo integration)

Click + Add to register a new piece of equipment. Use the Sync button to synchronize equipment data with the external fleet management system (Hiboo).

Hole Tolerances

Define acceptable tolerance thresholds for drilling accuracy on this site. For each metric (e.g., position, depth, azimuth, inclination), you can set:

  • As Designed maximum and minimum deviation — the range within which a hole is considered acceptably placed.
  • In Tolerance maximum and minimum deviation — a tighter range indicating ideal accuracy.

These thresholds are used to color-code holes on the map and in quality reports, highlighting deviations from the planned design.

KPI Display

Configure how KPIs are displayed for this site, including which KPIs are visible and their display order.

KPI Setup

Configure which Key Performance Indicators are tracked for this site. The KPI setup table shows the following columns:

At the bottom of the page, the Set predefined KPI config feature lets you apply a preset KPI profile from the Profil dropdown. Click Confirm to apply or Cancel to discard.

Operators

Orthophotos (Aerial Imagery)

Upload and manage orthophotos (aerial images) for the site. Orthophotos provide a geo-referenced photographic background layer on the interactive map.

To manage orthophotos:

  2. Uploaded orthophotos appear in a list with preview thumbnail, name, and creation date.
  3. Use the up/down arrows to change the display order (layering priority on the map).
  4. Toggle the visibility checkbox to show or hide a layer.
  5. Click the delete button to remove an orthophoto.

You can also initialize orthophotos from MapBox satellite imagery using the Bootstrap option.

From the projects list, two export options are available:

  • Export analysis data — Generates a CSV file containing all analysis/KPI data for selected projects within a specified date range.

  • Deliveries Log Report — A record of all explosive deliveries with optional filtering by delivery and a resume mode.

  • Feedback Report — A compilation of customer and supplier survey responses filtered by blast.

Select the report type, choose the projects, set the date range and time window, then click Download.

6. Blasts — Planning and Execution

Blasts (called "tirs" in French) are the core operational unit in Explore. A blast represents a planned and executed detonation event on a mining site.

Each blast is accessed via the project tree and has the following tabs: General, KPI, Design, Load, Holes, Deliveries, Vibrations, Fragmentation, Environment, and Drilling.

6.1 My Blasts List

Navigate to Operations > Projects, then select a project and click on the Blasts view, or access blasts directly via the project tree view. The blasts list displays:

Column Description
Name Blast name
Is Executed Checkbox indicating whether the blast has been detonated
Project Parent project name
Zone Zone where the blast is located
Planned Blast Date Scheduled detonation date
Actual Blast Date Real detonation date (filled after execution)

By default, the list shows only non-executed blasts, sorted by project name.

6.2 Creating a New Blast

To create a new blast, navigate to the project tree view and use the blast creation function. Provide the blast name, zone, planned date, and blast type. The blast is created in a draft state.

6.3 Blast Design

The Design tab is where the blast pattern is planned. The interface is divided into three panels:

Left panel — Quality data

A collapsible panel displaying quality metrics for the blast pattern. You can filter the view by:

  • State — Filter holes by their current status (actual, drilled, loaded, etc.)
  • Quality — Color-code holes by quality metrics with percentage values
  • Dates — Filter holes by drilling date (Day 1, Day 2, etc.)

Center panel — Map view

An interactive 2D map displaying the blast pattern layout. Holes are shown as points on the map, color-coded according to the selected filter. A status chart summarizes hole states visually.

Right panel — Holes list

A searchable table listing all holes in the blast pattern. Features include:

  • Search field to filter holes by name
  • Add Hole button to manually add a new hole to the pattern
  • Column settings button to toggle which columns are visible
  • Click on a row to select and highlight the hole on the map

Hole Positioning (Pattern)

Each hole in the blast pattern is defined by its planned position (X, Y coordinates or GPS), which determines its location on the bench. Holes can be added manually or imported from Expertab after field survey.

Geometric Parameters

Each blast hole has the following geometric properties:

Parameter Unit Description
Depth m Planned depth of the hole
Azimuth ° Horizontal angle from north
Inclination ° Vertical angle from horizontal
Diameter mm Drill hole diameter

Blast Polygons

Blast polygons define the geographic outline of the blast area. From the map view, you can create polygons of different types:

  • Drilled — Outline of the area that has been drilled
  • Muckpile — Outline of the resulting muckpile after detonation
  • Danger Zone — Safety exclusion perimeter

To create a polygon, click the polygon tool on the map, select the blast and polygon type, then draw the boundary.

6.4 Blast Loading

The Load tab manages the explosive charges assigned to each hole. The interface follows the same three-panel layout as the Design tab.

Filters

In addition to State, Quality, and Date filters, the loading view adds a Delivery filter, allowing you to color-code holes by which delivery provided their explosive materials.

Hole Charge Details

Select a hole to view its charge profile. The Hole Charge view displays a vertical bar visualization of the hole, showing:

  • Mine Type — The charge configuration type
  • Total weight (kg) — Sum of all explosive products in the hole
  • Tube length (m) — Total length of the hole
  • Charge segments — Stacked colored blocks representing each product, with product name, length, and proportion

Explosive Product Assignment

Products (bulk explosives, cartridges, detonators, detonating cord) are assigned to holes during the loading process. This can be done per-hole or in bulk via delivery records synced from Expertab.

6.5 Blast Environment

The Environment tab displays CO2 emission data for the blast, organized into four categories:

  • Transport — CO2 from moving explosive materials from factory to storage to site. Displayed per factory/storage route in kg.
  • Drilling — CO2 generated during the drilling phase, by drill type. Shows drilled meters, CO2 per meter, and total.
  • Detonation — CO2 from the explosive detonation itself, by product. Shows quantity, CO2 per unit, and total.
  • Production — CO2 from manufacturing the explosive products. Shows quantity, CO2 per unit, and total.

A donut chart on the left summarizes total CO2 (in kg) with a percentage breakdown by category.

6.6 3D Blast Visualization

The Holes tab provides a 3D view of the blast pattern using a WebGL canvas. Features include:

  • 3D rendering of all drill holes with depth, inclination, and charge visualization
  • Add Holes — Import hole data from files

7.6 Synchronization with Expertab (Field Tablet)

Expertab is the companion tablet application used by drill operators in the field. It provides:

  • Display of the blast pattern and hole locations
  • GPS-guided navigation to each hole position
  • Real-time recording of drill session data (depth, timestamps, pauses)
  • Geological observation entry per depth interval
  • Delivery record capture

Synchronization behavior:

  • Expertab operates in offline-first mode — data is captured locally even without connectivity.
  • When the tablet connects to the network, it syncs all captured data to the Explore server.
  • The sync protocol handles duplicates idempotently, so repeated syncs are safe.
  • Blast patterns only appear on Expertab when they are in an Approved status.
  • Synced data (drill sessions, geology logs, deliveries) appears in Explore after successful synchronization.

10. Vibration Monitoring

10.1 Seismograph Control Points

Seismograph control points are the physical locations where vibration monitoring instruments are placed. These are configured in the site setup (see Seismograph) and serve as reference points for assigning vibration records.

10.2 Recording and Viewing Vibrations

The Vibrations tab within a blast detail view displays all vibration records associated with that blast. Records can be filtered by:

  • Assigned Records — Records already linked to a control point
  • Unassigned Records — Records not yet assigned
  • All — Both assigned and unassigned

The list is paginated with navigation controls (First, Previous, page numbers, Next, Last) and displays the total record count.

Each record shows a summary with vibration measurements across three axes:

Channel Axis Description
V Vertical Vertical ground velocity
L Longitudinal Longitudinal ground velocity
T Transverse Transverse ground velocity
VS Vector Sum Combined vector magnitude

Additional measurements include Air Overpressure (in Pa and dB(L)).

10.3 Vibration Charts

Select a vibration record to view its detailed charts. Three chart types are available via tabs:

Signal tab

Displays the raw vibration waveform for each channel (Channel 1, 2, 3), showing the transducer type, axis orientation, and raw peak value in mm/s or Pa.

Frequency tab

Displays the FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) analysis for each channel, showing the frequency spectrum with peak frequency values in Hz.

Standard tab

Displays weighted vibration values according to a selectable weighting standard. Shows the PPV Max (Peak Particle Velocity maximum) in mm/s for each channel.

10.4 Assigning Records to a Blast

Vibration records from seismographs can be assigned to specific blasts. From the vibration detail page:

  1. Select one or more unassigned records.
  2. Click the Multi-assignment button or assign individually to a control point.
  3. The record is then linked to the blast and its distance to the blast can be recorded.

12. Workflows and Approvals

12.1 Workflow Principles in Explore

Workflows in Explore define the approval process that blasts and other operational objects must follow before execution. A workflow is a sequence of steps, each with conditions, validators, and document requirements. Workflows ensure that every blast goes through the proper review and authorization process before detonation.

12.2 Creating and Configuring a Workflow Template

Workflow templates can be managed at two levels:

  • Site level — Navigate to site setup > Workflow Templates
  • Region level — Navigate to region setup > Workflows

The workflow template list displays all available templates sorted by name. Click on a template to edit it, or use the Add button to create a new one.

12.3 Steps, Conditions, and Validators

Each workflow template consists of ordered steps. For each step, you can define:

  • Step name — A descriptive label for the approval stage
  • Conditions — Requirements that must be met before the step can be completed
  • Validators — Users or roles authorized to approve the step
  • Transitions — Rules governing progression to the next step

12.4 Tracking Workflow Progress

When a blast is associated with a workflow, its progress through the steps is visible in the blast detail view. Each step shows its current status (pending, in progress, approved, rejected) and any associated documents.

12.5 Document Management per Step

Each workflow step can have associated documents. The file explorer component allows:

  • Go to Root / Go to Parent — Navigate the folder structure
  • Add Files — Upload documents (PDF, images, etc.)
  • Create Folder — Organize documents into folders
  • Rename — Rename files or folders
  • Download — Download selected items
  • Cut / Paste — Move items between folders
  • Delete — Remove selected items
  • Select All — Select all items in current folder
  • Sort — Toggle alphabetical sort order

The file table shows: file name, extension, size (MB), and creation date. Folders are listed first, followed by files. A footer displays information about the selected items (count, total size).

  • Machine health alerts and status
  • Equipment list synchronization

Configuration requires an API key and base URL. The integration degrades gracefully when Hiboo is unavailable.

MapBox

Mapping and geospatial visualization provider. Explore uses MapBox for:

  • Interactive 2D site maps
  • Base map rendering (satellite, street, terrain)
  • Drill hole and blast pattern visualization
  • Layer management and styling
  • Token management is handled server-side for security

Keycloak

Open-source identity and access management platform. Keycloak provides:

  • User authentication (login/logout)
  • JWT token issuance and validation
  • Role-based access control
  • Password management (creation, reset)
  • Session management and token refresh

The Keycloak realm used by Explore is vertex. Users are managed through the Explore admin interface, which communicates with Keycloak behind the scenes.
